Allocation conditions (how "Suggest" works)
  • Allocation starts on the Start date and spans the Duration (weeks) you set. End date is derived from these.
  • Weekly allocation per person is flat, from 2h up to 32h per week, in 2h steps.
  • One person works at most 32h/week. If a role needs more, it is split across multiple people.
  • Max people per role: Delivery Manager = 1, Project Manager = 1 (2 only when Data Science is maxed at 3 people × 32h/week), hands-on roles = 3.
  • The suggestion never allocates more hours than the breakdown — it matches exactly or falls slightly short (rounded down to 2h).
  • Too short a duration (doesn't fit even at max people) → allocates the maximum and warns about leftover hours.
  • Too long a duration → a person drops to the 2h minimum, placed on the first weeks needed (later weeks stay empty).
  • Total Values Match? is TRUE only when the allocated hours equal the breakdown for every role.
