How weekly values & calculations work
Weekly cells are days per week (0.25–4, multiples of 0.25). Blank = not allocated (excluded from FTE average). Hours = days × 8. FTE = average of filled weeks ÷ 5. PIR groups roles: DS = Data Scientist + Data Analyst; RiskC = Risk Consultant + Risk&AI Manager; ENG (TS) = Software Engineer + Technical Lead + Solution Architect + Engineering Manager.
